# Written source offer for packaged bubblewrap binaries

For at least three years after a Coda distribution containing the adjacent `bwrap` binary is made, the Coda project offers any recipient the complete machine-readable corresponding source for that binary, including build scripts and distribution patches, for no more than the physical cost of conveying the source.

Request the source through <https://github.com/rita152/coda/issues> and include the architecture, `provenance.json`, and SHA-256 digest shipped with the binary. Redistributors are responsible for preserving this offer or directly accompanying their artifact with its complete corresponding source under the GNU Library General Public License version 2.
